Our Top 4 Bean Coffee

1.Arabica (Coffea arabica)

  • Flavor: Smooth, mild, often slightly sweet with hints of fruit, chocolate, or nuts.
  • Caffeine: Lower than other types (~1.2%)
  • Growing altitude: High altitudes (600–2,000 meters)
  • Climate: Cooler tropical climates
  • Notes: Most popular type (about 60–70% of global production), higher quality, more expensive.

2. Robusta (Coffea canephora)

  • Flavor: Strong, bitter, earthy, sometimes with a nutty or burnt rubber taste.
  • Caffeine: Higher (~2.2%)
  • Growing altitude: Lower elevations (sea level to 800 meters)
  • Climate: Hotter, more resistant to pests and diseases
  • Notes: Cheaper to grow, used in instant coffee or espresso blends for extra "kick" and crema.

3. Liberica (Coffea liberica)

  • Flavor: Woody, smoky, floral, bold, with a unique aftertaste.
  • Caffeine: Medium (~1.2–1.5%)
  • Growing altitude: Low to medium
  • Climate:Grows well in Southeast Asia and the Philippines
  • Notes: Large, irregular-shaped beans; not as common, but gaining niche interest.

4. Excelsa (Coffea excelsa)

  • Flavor: Tart, fruity, complex, and often used to add depth to blends.
  • Caffeine:Similar to Arabica
  • Growing altitude: Medium to high
  • Climate:Southeast Asia
  • Notes: Technically a variety of Liberica, but often classified separately due to its distinct taste.
