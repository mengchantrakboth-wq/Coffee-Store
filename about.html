About Us

Rooted in the craft, brewed with care.

Our Story

From one cart to a corner everyone knows

We started in 2015 with a single espresso cart and a stubborn belief that a good cup shouldn't be complicated. Today we roast in small batches, source directly from four regions, and still pull every shot by hand — no shortcuts, just better coffee.

Every bean on our shelf is picked for what it tastes like in the cup, not just what it costs. That's the whole philosophy, really.

What We Stand For

Direct Sourcing

We buy straight from growers in Cambodia, Ethiopia, Colombia, and Vietnam, paying fair prices for beans we're proud to roast.

Small-Batch Roasting

Every batch is roasted in-house, in small quantities, so every bag is fresh when it reaches your cup.

Community First

Our shops are built as a place to sit, work, and talk — not just to grab a cup and go.
